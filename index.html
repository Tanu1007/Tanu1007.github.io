About

I'm Tanvi Patel, a backend engineer and AI researcher based in New Delhi, India. I studied Electronics & Communication Engineering at NIT Surat, where I developed a deep interest in the intersection of embedded systems, computer vision, and intelligent machines.

At Airtel X Labs, I spent three years building the communication and payments infrastructure used by millions across Africa and India — scaling messaging systems to 3500 TPS, migrating core databases, and leading cross-border deployments across 10 countries.

My long-term goal is to pursue advanced work in Physical AI and embodied agents — building systems that perceive, reason, and act in the real world. I'm interested in the full stack: from low-level perception pipelines to high-level reasoning architectures.

When I'm not writing code or reading research papers, I paint. I also mentor junior engineers and students, and have coordinated placements for 200+ students at NIT Surat as Training & Placement Coordinator.
